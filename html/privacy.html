Privacy Policy

This page explains how Insta Crown handles information, browser storage and third-party services when you use our website.

Important: This policy describes the intended privacy practices of Insta Crown. Actual data handling depends on which website features and external services are enabled in the deployed version. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through contact forms or URL fields.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Insta Crown. We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information may be handled when you visit or use our website.

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please stop using the website and its services.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the website or its service providers may process the following categories of information:

Information You Provide

  • Name and email address entered into a contact form.
  • Messages, questions, suggestions and feedback you choose to submit.
  • Instagram URLs or usernames entered into a tool.
  • Information you choose to provide when using an account feature, if enabled.

Technical Information

  • Browser type and device information.
  • Basic website usage and diagnostic information.
  • Network and error information processed by hosting or service providers.
Insta Crown does not ask you to provide your Instagram password. Never enter your Instagram password into this website.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and displaying website features.
  • Processing requests submitted through available tools.
  • Responding to support messages and feedback.
  • Maintaining account features if enabled.
  • Detecting errors, misuse and security problems.
  • Improving website usability and performance.
  • Meeting applicable legal requirements.

We do not claim that every listed feature is currently active. The actual processing depends on the services configured in the live website.

4. Browser Storage and Cookies

Some website features may use browser storage, including local storage or session storage, to remember preferences and recent activity.

  • Theme preferences such as dark mode.
  • Recent tool activity or history.
  • Temporary interface settings.

Browser storage is generally kept on your device. You can clear website data through your browser settings, although doing so may reset saved preferences and history.

Hosting providers or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Firebase Services

Insta Crown may use Google Firebase services for features such as authentication and database storage. These services are only active if properly configured in the deployed website.

If Firebase services are enabled, relevant information may be processed by Google according to Google's terms and privacy documentation.

  • Authentication information for account features.
  • Database records associated with enabled features.
  • Technical information needed to operate the services.

Do not assume that information is stored in Firebase unless that feature has actually been connected and activated.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party services for hosting, fonts, authentication, media processing or other functionality.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and service terms. Insta Crown does not control every practice of an independent provider.

Please review the privacy documentation of any external service you choose to use.

7. Information Sharing

We do not claim to sell your personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary for operating enabled services or where legally required.

  • With service providers supporting website operations.
  • When required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the website.
  • When you specifically choose to interact with an external service.

8. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, internet connection or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keep your own accounts secure and avoid sharing passwords, payment credentials or confidential information through public forms.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the features you use, you may have choices regarding your personal information.

  • You can stop using the website at any time.
  • You can clear locally stored website data through your browser.
  • You can choose not to submit optional information.
  • You can contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

If an account or database feature is enabled, additional account-specific deletion or correction procedures may apply.

10. Children's Privacy

Insta Crown is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

11. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms and service providers. Those websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, service providers or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date. Please review this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact the Insta Crown team.

Email: support@instacrown.com
